James Fleming is a **Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in Intelligent Control Systems** in the Wolfson School of Mechanical, Electrical and Manufacturing Engineering at Loughborough University, where he leads the Intelligent Control Group.

He obtained his MEng and DPhil degrees in 2012 and 2016 respectively from the University of Oxford, where he studied control engineering and developed algorithms for model predictive control of uncertain state-space systems. From 2016 to 2019 he was a Research Fellow at the University of Southampton, developing driver models and optimal control algorithms for the G-Active (Green, Adaptive ConTrol of Interconnected VEhicles) project.

He holds an EPSRC New Investigator Award on learning safety-critical model predictive controllers for autonomous systems.
