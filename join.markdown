## PhD study

We supervise PhD projects in model predictive control, learning-based control,
autonomous vehicles and related areas. Good candidates usually have a strong degree
in engineering, mathematics, physics or computer science and an interest in both
theory and practice.

To enquire, email [j.fleming@lboro.ac.uk](mailto:j.fleming@lboro.ac.uk) with:
*   your CV and degree transcripts
*   a short paragraph on the research you would like to do
*   how you plan to fund your studies, if known

Information on applying and funding is available from
[Loughborough University's doctoral college](https://www.lboro.ac.uk/study/postgraduate/research-degrees/).

## Postdoctoral researchers and fellowships

We are happy to support strong applicants for fellowships (e.g. UKRI, Royal Academy
of Engineering, Leverhulme, Marie Skłodowska-Curie). Please get in touch well before
the deadline to discuss a proposal.
